# Net Impact

Lighthouses strive for a specific goal: **cost-effective impact** at **scale** — improving both the depth of impact per beneficiary and its breadth across beneficiaries, per unit of cost.

Your **net impact** is a function of how well you achieve that depth and breadth, and at what cost.

 :::info
**Net Impact = Depth of Impact x Breadth of Coverage - Cost**
:::

Collect data at every touchpoint of the programme and the beneficiary (1.1), turn it into metrics that progress from engagement through proximal outcomes to development outcomes (1.2), and commit organisationally — with the systems and tools to match — to improving them (1.3). Pillar 1 produces one output that pillar 2 consumes: a defensible statement of what is currently binding.

## Improve Execution

Treat the programme as a product, with research, data, field, ops and engineering improving it together (2.1); work with beneficiaries deeply enough to design a good experience and pressure-test ideas against how they actually live (2.2); and estimate the impact of an idea well enough to decide whether it is worth scaling up (2.3). This is where depth comes from.

## Scale Operations

Some scale happens in-platform on the capabilities above, but most of it happens through distribution partners with access to a large beneficiary base. That requires a platform a third party can actually operate (3.1), the first two pillars made structurally unavoidable in operations (3.2), and a repeatable onboarding path covering systems integration, beneficiary onboarding, adaptation, training and compliance (3.3).
