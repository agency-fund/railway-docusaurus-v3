# Net Impact

Net Impact = Depth of Impact X Breadth of Coverage - Cost

## Depth of Impact

While its important for social sector orgs to report their impact metric like increase in household income, its equally important for them and their funders to understand how that impact is achieved.

- L4: Impact
- L3: Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices (KAP)
- L2: Engagement
- L1: Tech Platform

What are the top-level metrics that you publish in your annual report?

What are the intermediate metrics that contribute to those metrics?

Causality: Confirmed vs Assumed – Are you sure what really contributes?

Who owns which metric?

### User

 A user is anyone who interacts with your system. If you have field teams delivering services to beneficiaries, they’re both your users!

## Breadth of Coverage

- Direct reach through programs
- Indirect reach through partners

## Cost

- Total Cost
- Marginal Cost per Engagement
