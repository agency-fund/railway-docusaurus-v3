# Welcome

There are many organizations in the social sector, but very few operate with high standards of impact measurement, growth, and scale; we consider them **lighthouses**.

A lighthouse is a social sector organization that supports public sector scale-up of innovations while continuously iterating, evaluating, and evolving toward greater cost-effectiveness. They have demonstrated ability to use technology and data to [track metrics](measure/getting-started.mdx), [improve operations](improve/getting-started.mdx), and [scale](scale/getting-started.mdx) their [net impact](net-impact.mdx).

This knowledge base serves as a living repository of dos, donts, tools and case studies that can help your organization become a lighthouse. In particular, lighthouses excel at **capabilities** like product management, experimentation, marketing, and AI evals that enable them to operate efficiently and effectively.
