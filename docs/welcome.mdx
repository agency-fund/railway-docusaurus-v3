# Welcome

There are many organizations in the social sector, but very few operate with high standards of impact measurement, growth, and scale; we consider them **lighthouses**.

A lighthouse is a social sector organization that supports public sector scale-up of innovations while continuously iterating, evaluating, and evolving toward greater cost-effectiveness. They have demonstrated ability to use technology and data to [measure](observability/metrics-dashboard.mdx) their performance, [improve](improve-and-scale/research-and-learning/getting-started.mdx) their operations, and [scale](improve-and-scale/go-to-market/getting-started.mdx) their [net impact](net-impact.mdx).

This knowledge base serves as a living repository of *dos*, *donts*, tools, and case studies that can help your organization become a lighthouse.
