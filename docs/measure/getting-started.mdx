# Getting Started

1. Identify your [User(s)](user-journey#who-isare-your-users)
2. Map their [User Journey(s)](user-journey#map-the-user-journey)
3. Decide what [engagement](user-journey#l2-engagement) and [KAP](user-journey#l3-knowledge-attitudes-and-practices-kap) to track in the [User Funnel(s)](user-journey)
4. Present top-level metrics in a [Dashboard](metrics-dashboard)
5. Compute your [Net Impact](../net-impact)
